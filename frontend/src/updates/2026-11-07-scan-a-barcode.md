caption: v0.17.0
date: 2026-11-07

---

When you add an item, tap the **barcode button** next to the food search and point your camera at a barcode. Burrow reads it by itself and fills in the name.

You pick the food type from a short list of guesses. The next time you scan that barcode, Burrow remembers the food, name and size you chose.

No camera? Choose a photo of the barcode instead.
