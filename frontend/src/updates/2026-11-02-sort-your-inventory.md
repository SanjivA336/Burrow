---
title: Sort your inventory
caption: v0.15.0
date: 2026-11-02
---

Your inventory list can now be **sorted**. Tap the up-and-down arrows next to the search bar:

- **Recently used:** what you opened lately comes first (the default)
- **Name, Expiry date, Amount left** or **Newest added**

**Download data** moved to **Settings, then Burrow**, and now saves everything in stock.

Small touches: a flatter look with fewer shadows, outlined food-type dots so pale colors show up, and chart lines that start with your accent color.
