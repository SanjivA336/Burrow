---
title: Add several at once
caption: v0.16.0
date: 2026-11-05
---

Bought three cartons of milk? When you add an item, set **Containers** to 3. Burrow makes **three separate items**, so each one is used up and expires on its own.

- Type the amount in **one** container. A line under it shows the total.
- For the cost, pick **Total** (what they all cost together) or **Each**. A total is split to the cent between the containers.

The inventory list also has a **Group** button next to Sort: group by **storage location** or by **food type**.

Small touches: the order form's fields are all the same height and text size, and the bottom tab bar no longer gets cut off on some iPhones when Burrow is added to the home screen.
