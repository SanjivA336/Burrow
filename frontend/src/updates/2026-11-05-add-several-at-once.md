---
title: Add several at once
caption: v0.16.0
date: 2026-11-05
---

Bought three cartons of milk? When you add an item, set **Containers** to 3. Burrow makes **three separate items**, so each one is used up and expires on its own.

For the cost, pick **Total** or **Each**.

The inventory list also has a **Group** button next to Sort: group by **storage location** or **food type**.
