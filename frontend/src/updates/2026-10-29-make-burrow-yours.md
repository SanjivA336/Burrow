Burrow now opens in **light mode**. Change how it looks in **Settings, then Appearance**:

- **Theme:** Light, Dark or Match device
- **Accent color:** green, blue, purple, red, orange or yellow
- **Display size:** Small, Medium or Large

Each choice is saved on the device you are using. This card is new too: a small dot shows when there is something you have not read.
