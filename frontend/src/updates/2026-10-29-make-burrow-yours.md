Burrow now opens in a light look by default, and you can change how it looks from **Settings, then Appearance**.

## Pick your look

- **Theme:** Light, Dark, or Match device, which follows your phone or computer.
- **Accent color:** green, blue, purple, red, orange or yellow.
- **Display size:** Small, Medium or Large, if text is hard to read.

Each of these is saved on the device you are using, so your phone and your computer can look different.

## Also new

- **Updates:** this card. Announcements and short articles will show up here, and a small dot appears when there is something you have not read.
- **Tidier buttons:** the Shopping List and Balances buttons now sit in a single row.
